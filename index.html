Master's thesis · University of Freiburg · since July 2026 · in progress

SAM4D: from single image 3D to temporally coherent video

SAM 3D Objects reconstructs an object from one image. Run it frame by frame on a video and the geometry jitters, because every frame is reconstructed in isolation. My thesis analyses where that incoherence comes from and extends the model toward reconstructions that hold together across a sequence.

It is built as a research codebase rather than a pile of scripts: a sam4d command line tool covering dataset download, preprocessing, config and data validation and multi modal visualisation, driven by config over the CO3D dataset, with an environment doctor so experiments reproduce.

Python · PyTorch · SAM 3D · 4D reconstruction · CO3D · Multi view geometry

The repository stays private while the thesis is under way. I am glad to walk through it in a conversation.

University of Freiburg · Oct 2025 to Jun 2026

DreMa: a Gaussian Splatting world model on a real robot

I took the DreMa framework (ICLR 2025) out of simulation and onto a Franka Emika Panda: automated RGBD data collection with Bézier arch, orbit and line scan patterns, camera to robot calibration, Gaussian Splatting reconstruction, augmented demonstration generation, and a PerAct policy trained and then deployed back on the hardware.

I replaced the original DEVA segmentation backend with Meta's SAM 3, including a visually prompted video tracker that propagates masks across every frame, and rebuilt point cloud aggregation and mesh generation for the noisier scans you get in a real lab.

Python · CUDA · Gaussian Splatting · SAM 3 · ROS · Open3D · OpenCV · PerAct
