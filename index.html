SAM4D — from single-image 3D to temporally coherent video

SAM 3D Objects reconstructs an object from one image, but run it frame by frame on a video and the geometry jitters — each frame is reconstructed in isolation. My thesis analyses where that temporal incoherence comes from and extends the model toward reconstructions that stay consistent across a sequence.

Built as a proper research codebase rather than a pile of scripts: a sam4d CLI covering dataset download, preprocessing, config/data validation and multi-modal visualisation, with a config-driven pipeline over CO3D and an environment doctor so experiments reproduce.

DreMa — Gaussian-Splatting world model on a real robot

Took the DreMa framework (ICLR 2025) from simulation to a complete real-robot pipeline on a Franka Emika Panda: automated RGB-D data collection with Bézier-arch, orbit and line-scan patterns, camera–robot calibration, Gaussian-Splatting reconstruction, augmented demonstration generation, and a PerAct policy trained and deployed back on the hardware.

Replaced the original DEVA segmentation backend with Meta’s SAM 3, including a visual-prompted video tracker that propagates masks across every frame, and rebuilt point-cloud aggregation and mesh generation for noisier real-world scans.

2026 · Applied ML challenge

AlphaEarth deforestation detection

Pixel-level deforestation detection on unseen satellite tiles, delivered as polygons with a confidence and a year. Rather than reach for a heavy segmentation network, I measured per-pixel drift in yearly AlphaEarth embeddings against a 2020 baseline and fed those change features to a single gradient-boosted classifier — trained on a fusion of three noisy weak-label sources rather than any one of them, since no ground-truth masks existed.

The whole pipeline is driven by one Hydra config, so every run is reproducible and controllable end to end.
